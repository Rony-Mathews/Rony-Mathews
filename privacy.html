Privacy Policy for Image Gallery

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Overview

Image Gallery is a personal image-management application operated by YOUR_NAME.

This policy explains how the application accesses, uses, stores and shares information, including information obtained from Google Drive and Google Sheets.

Google data accessed

The application accesses a designated Google Drive folder to upload, store and verify original image files.

The application accesses a designated Google Sheet to create and update image records. These records may include titles, descriptions, tags, people, collections, dates, locations, ratings, file information and publication status.

How Google data is used

Google Drive and Google Sheets information is used only to provide the image-upload, management, review and publishing features requested by the authorized administrator.

The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

Published content

When an image is approved for publication, optimized copies of the image and selected metadata may be stored using Cloudflare R2 and made accessible through the gallery.

Users should treat approved and published images as potentially publicly accessible.

Storage and security

Backend OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted Cloudflare Worker secrets. Administrative credentials are not intentionally included in publicly accessible gallery records.

Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials and connected services from unauthorized access.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.

Information is processed by infrastructure providers, including Google and Cloudflare, only as necessary to operate the application.

Data retention and deletion

Images and metadata are retained until the administrator deletes them or requests their deletion.

Google access can be revoked through the connected Google Account's security settings. Deletion requests can also be submitted using the contact address below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's data-handling practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will identify the latest revision.

Contact
